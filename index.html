The gap

Where today's options fall short

Hiring your way there is slow and costly, and most outside support covers only one side.

Today

  • Most providers know only one side of the PE relationship
  • Fund-level and company-level finance run on disconnected processes
  • Technology and AI treated as an add-on, if at all
  • In-house builds are slow, costly and leave key-person risk

The gap

  • Few partners understand what both sides need
  • The two sides need to connect, through effective bridging or a single consolidated system
  • PE Firms need real-time portfolio data to meet LP reporting demands
  • Growing companies need senior finance capability without the hiring cost
