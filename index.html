Questions we hear

Before we talk

Are you there for management or for the PE owner?

Whoever engages us. At a Portfolio Company we work for management, not around it: we strengthen your team and help it deliver what the owner needs. Each engagement stands alone, with its own team, scope and data. [Partners to confirm conflict policy]

How is this different from a fund administrator?

An administrator processes and records. We lead: we own the reporting, the forecasts and the judgment calls a CFO makes, and we oversee your administrator so the handoffs work. We work alongside your administrator, auditors, tax preparers and lenders, never in place of them.

Are you just two people?

Every engagement is led by a founder, with our PE finance team in reporting, controls and FP&A, and trusted partners for tax and valuation.

How quickly can you start?

Senior leaders are ready now. There's no executive search, so most engagements start within weeks of our first discussion, not months.

How do you charge?

Ongoing work is a fixed monthly fee. Projects are fixed or capped fees agreed up front. You pay for the capability you need, not a full-time team, and you can scale up or down as needs change.

How do you handle our data and use AI?

We work within your systems and security policies, alongside your auditors and valuation providers, never in place of them. We fix the process first, then use AI to speed up what remains, and a finance expert reviews everything that leaves our hands.
