Before you call

Who do you work for if a sponsor's and a portfolio company's interests differ?

Whoever engages us. Each engagement stands alone, with its own team, scope and data. We don't share one client's information with another, and if a real conflict comes up, for example over a valuation or a covenant, we disclose it and step back. [Partners to confirm policy]

How is this different from a fund administrator?

An administrator processes and records. We lead: we own the reporting, the forecasts and the judgment calls a CFO makes, and we oversee your administrator so the handoffs work. Most of our PE firm clients use both.

How quickly can you start?

Senior leaders are ready now. There's no executive search, so most engagements start within weeks of our first call, not months.

How do you charge?

Ongoing work is a fixed monthly fee. Projects are fixed or capped fees agreed up front. You pay for the capability you need, not a full-time team, and you can scale up or down as needs change.

How do you handle our data and use AI?

AI speeds up the routine work, and a person reviews everything that leaves our hands. [Partners to confirm: where client data is stored, no training of models on client data, security standards]
